# Understanding Artificial Intelligence

### The Science of Evaluating AI Systems — From Machine Learning Foundations to Modern AI Engineering

**Anthony Vinokur** · 2026 · free to read

<p align="center"><img src="assets/cover.png" alt="Book cover" width="320"></p>

AI systems are easy to demo and hard to trust. This book explains how to tell the difference.

It starts with how models actually learn and generate text, then treats AI evaluation as a measurement science: what a pass rate really tells you, how to put an honest interval around it, why a confident AI judge is not necessarily a calibrated one, how to test retrieval systems and agents by what they *do* rather than what they say, and how to turn results into evidence that a release decision can rest on.

Every chapter ends with a worked example small enough to check by hand.
